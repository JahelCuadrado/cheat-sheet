# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-24

First release for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric). Everything you keep looking up on the wiki, one click
away in the pause menu.

- Book button in the pause menu, and an optional key to open the cheat sheet while playing.
- Ores: height charts for the Overworld and the Nether with the best height to dig at for each
  ore, your current height, and biome bonuses with each biome in miniature.
- Enchantments: every enchantment grouped by the gear it goes on, with its highest level, treasure
  ones and curses marked and what each one does on hover; plus a step-by-step anvil guide for
  putting many books on one tool.
- Potions: a brewing chart from water bottle to every potion, with the redstone, glowstone,
  fermented spider eye, gunpowder and dragon's breath upgrades.
- Armor Trims: where to find each design, on an armor stand in a full netherite set with its
  template and the structure in miniature, how to copy it, and every design in every material.
- Music Discs: every disc by source, with its length and comparator signal; places in miniature
  and the mobs that drop discs.
- Dyes: the 16 dyes with their flowers, items and mixes.
- Villagers: all trades by level with each villager in its work clothes, its workstation and that
  block's recipe, the wandering trader with his llamas, and piglin bartering odds.
- Food: foods ranked by hunger filled, with saturation, best picks and side effects.
- Beacon & Conduit: pyramid sizes, ranges, effects and payments, and a step-by-step conduit
  tutorial with its recipe, where its materials come from and how to build its frame.
- Farming: sugar cane layouts, crop fields, pumpkins and melons, cactus, and breeding: what each
  animal eats, where to find it, how to get it, and its variants with the biomes they come from.
- Click any card to zoom in on it, with more details.
- Crisp at any GUI scale: the sheet picks its own size so the whole tab fits on screen.
- Settings screen (also from Mod Menu): pause menu button on or off, tab to open on, sheet size
  and key binding.
- Available in English, Español (España), Español (México), Português (Brasil), Português
  (Portugal), Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Nederlands, Polski, Türkçe, Русский, Українська,
  简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語 and 한국어.
